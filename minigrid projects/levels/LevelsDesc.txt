The purpose of this folder is to store grid level layouts, generated by the generate_level.py script
The initial train_ppo_var script trains on randomly generated grids however each episode the grid
is new and different, the training has not gone particularly well, and this may be due to the agent
not having enough time to learn one grid environment before moving onto the next, so in this folder
level layouts will be stored and can be ran repeatedly to ensure proper training.

Visibility either 7 or 11

stage1 - 20x16 grid with 1 wall and 1 door
stage1Test - test grid for above structure
stage1small - 16x12 grid with 1 wall and 1 door
stage1_2doors - 20x16 grid with 1 wall and 2 doors
stage1_2doorsTest - test grid for above structure
stage2 - 20x16 grid with 2 walls and 1 door
stage2small - 16x12 grid with 2 walls and 1 door
